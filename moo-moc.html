What is MOO Imbalance?

Market-on-Open (MOO) imbalances represent the net difference between buy and sell orders queued for execution at market open.

  • Published ~15 minutes before market open
  • Positive = more buy orders than sell orders
  • Large imbalances often predict gap-up or gap-down opens
  • Mag 7 imbalance shows mega-cap tech sentiment

What is MOC Imbalance?

Market-on-Close (MOC) imbalances show institutional order flow heading into the closing auction.

  • Published ~30 minutes before market close
  • Negative = heavy sell pressure into close
  • Nasdaq imbalance drives tech stock direction
  • All data analyzed in real-time by Claude AI
