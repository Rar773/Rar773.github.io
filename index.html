// FAQ

Questions &
Answers

Who are we?

InstantNews is an automated breaking-news service for traders, running 24/7. We publish market-moving financial and geopolitical headlines the moment they break.

Where does the news come from?

From established financial news wires, the Federal Reserve's own website and selected news channels. When we pass on a story from another channel, we name the source.

Telegram or X — what's the difference?

Telegram (@FastestNewsFeed) gets every headline. X (@InstantNews73) gets the most important ones: high alerts, economic data, earnings, imbalances and our daily brief.

What is HIGH ALERT?

Our maximum-urgency label for events with immediate market impact — rate decisions, military strikes, circuit breakers, defaults and similar shocks.

How is AI used?

Headlines are published as they are. AI (Claude by Anthropic) formats economic data and writes the short analysis for MOO/MOC, CFTC, the daily brief and the Sunday outlook. Numbers are always taken from the source data.

Is this financial advice?

No. InstantNews provides news and information only. Nothing we publish is financial advice. Always do your own research before making investment decisions.
