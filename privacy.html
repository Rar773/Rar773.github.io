Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

In short

We do not use cookies, we do not track you and we do not sell or share personal data. This website works without you giving us any personal information.

Who is responsible

InstantNews (instantnews24.net, Telegram @FastestNewsFeed, X @InstantNews73) is run by the InstantNews team. Contact: send a message to our Telegram bot @InstantNewsLiveBot — it reaches us directly.

What happens when you visit this website

  • Hosting. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). The news data shown on the pages is loaded from our server hosted by Railway (Railway Corp.). Like every web server, these providers process your IP address and basic browser information for a short time to deliver the pages and keep them secure. We do not store your IP address.
  • Page counter. To know which pages are read, the site sends us only the name of the page you opened (for example “/news”). We store a daily total per page. We store no IP address, no device or browser information and no identifier, so this count cannot be linked to you.
  • No cookies, no local tracking. We do not set cookies and we do not use analytics or advertising services. That is why there is no cookie banner.
  • Fonts. All fonts are stored on our own website. Your browser does not contact Google or any other font service.

The legal basis for hosting and the anonymous page count is our legitimate interest in providing a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Telegram and X

When you follow @FastestNewsFeed on Telegram or @InstantNews73 on X, the privacy policies of Telegram and X apply. We see only what these platforms show to any channel owner (for example the number of followers). If you send a message to our Telegram bot, it is forwarded to us so that we can reply. We do not copy it anywhere else.

News content

Headlines are collected from public news sources and published automatically. Some analysis texts are written with the help of AI (Claude by Anthropic); only news data, never visitor data, is sent for this.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Because we do not store personal data about visitors, in most cases there will be nothing to hand over — but you can always ask us at the contact above.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
